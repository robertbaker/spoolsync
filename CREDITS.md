## Service Providers

Supported tools, from the registry in `packages/spoolsync-core/src/core/services.ts`.

- [Spoolman / Spoolman-NG](https://github.com/Donkie/Spoolman): Self-hosted spool database with a REST API, used by Klipper setups.
- [Spoolstock](https://www.spoolstock.com): Filament and print management app for phones and tablets.
- [Spoolio](https://spoolio.net): Hosted, installable web app with label scanning.
- [TigerTag](https://tigertag.io): Open RFID filament format with a Studio app and cloud sync.
- [3DFilamentProfiles](https://3dfilamentprofiles.com): Filament database with a personal My Spools inventory.
- [Bambuddy](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy): Self-hosted control panel for Bambu Lab printers with AMS inventory.
- [FilaMeter](https://filameter.com): Local-first, open-source inventory that stores data in the browser.
- [Filadex](https://github.com/the-luap/filadex): Self-hosted filament collection manager.
- [FilamentDB](https://github.com/Pixelplanet/FilamentDB): Self-hosted spool database with a REST API.
- [SpoolMaster](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spool-master/id6757678949): Mac and iOS stock tracker that exports an Excel workbook.
- [Spool Hoarder](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spool-hoarder/id6760682447): Mac and iOS filament inventory with HueForge library export.
- [Spool (Mac and iOS app)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spool-filament-tracker/id6756892049): Mac and iOS app that keeps spools, prints and printers.
- [SpoolKeep](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spoolkeep/id6746877203): Mac and iOS app that counts spools rather than weighing them.
